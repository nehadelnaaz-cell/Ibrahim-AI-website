AI Customer Support Assistant

An AI-powered support chatbot prototype created for a matrimonial platform. It helps users find answers to common questions and understand how to get support.

What it can help with

  • Answering frequently asked questions about memberships and platform features.
  • Explaining the difference between free and paid account capabilities.
  • Guiding users through common login and account-access questions.
  • Providing structured responses to general troubleshooting queries.

How it was built

The prototype uses Python and Streamlit for the app experience, the Gemini API for AI responses, and prompt engineering to guide the assistant's behavior.
